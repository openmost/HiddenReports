## Changelog

### v5.0.1

- Translated into 12 languages
- New Marketplace cover and screenshots
- Shorter Marketplace description that fits the plugin cards, and campaign parameters on the Openmost links of the README.

### v5.0.0

- Hide reports from the reporting menu of a website (website admins)
- Hide reports from the reporting menu of all websites (super users)
- Every item of the reporting menu can be hidden: core and plugin reports, Custom Reports, Custom Dimensions and widgets such as Transitions or the real-time map
- Collapsible categories with a switch to hide or show a whole category at once
- Hidden reports stay archived and available through the API, exports, scheduled reports and dashboards
